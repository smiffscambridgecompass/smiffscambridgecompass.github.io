🍺 Smiff’s best beer in Cambridge

Where to go when the beer matters: real ale, craft taps, brewery taprooms and a couple of proper back-street locals.

1 The Cambridge Blue

Up to 12 real ales on at once, plus regular beer festivals.

85-87 Gwydir Street, CB1 2LG

See it on the map, and what’s on ›

2 The Free Press

Award-winning beer in a tiny, historic back-street pub.

7 Prospect Row, CB1 1DU

See it on the map, and what’s on ›

3 Calverley's Brewery Tap

Drink it where it's brewed, in the brewery's own taproom.

23A Hooper Street, CB1 2NZ

See it on the map, and what’s on ›

4 Handsome Prince

Baron Brewing's taproom: 18 craft taps and a bottle shop.

6-8 King Street, CB1 1LN

See it on the map, and what’s on ›

5 Old Ticket Office

5 hand pumps and 16 keg taps at the station, with regular tap takeovers.

Cambridge Railway Station, CB1 2JH

6 Pint Shop

Craft beer on Peas Hill, right in the middle of town.

10 Peas Hill, CB2 3PP

See it on the map, and what’s on ›

7 The Elm Tree

Belgian beers in a proper back-street local.

16A Orchard Street, CB1 1JT

8 King Street Brew House

Beer brewed on site on King Street.

1 King Street, CB1 1LH

9 The Maypole

A long-standing favourite that runs its own beer festivals.

20A Portugal Place, CB5 8AF

40 Mawson Road, CB1 2EA

Smiff’s Cambridge Compass is a free map of what’s on in Cambridge pubs, made by a local pub-goer. Listings come from venue websites, CAMRA and event listings, and are checked twice a week (last checked Fri 20 Nov 2026). Times and deals can change, so check with the pub before a big night out.

Run a pub or venue? Send your events to smiffscompass@gmail.com and they’ll go on the map for free, and get a free badge for your website.
