Add it to Gmail (on a computer)

  1. In Gmail, click the cog, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Smiff".
  3. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick "Smiff" for new emails and for replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes.
