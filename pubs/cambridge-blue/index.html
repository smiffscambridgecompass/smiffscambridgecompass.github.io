The story behind The Cambridge Blue

For a century this was the Dew Drop Inn. Its first known licensee, in 1871, was Thomas Stratton, a dairyman.

In the 1880s landlord Joseph Strange also ran a milk round. In 1889 alone he was fined for "furious driving" on Hills Road, his horse threw a boy who broke his arm, and his milk cart knocked down a street sweeper.

Sources: Capturing Cambridge
