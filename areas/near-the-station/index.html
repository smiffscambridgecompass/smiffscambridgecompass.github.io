Smiff’s Cambridge Compass is a free map of what’s on in Cambridge pubs, made by a local pub-goer. Listings come from venue websites, CAMRA and event listings, and are checked twice a week (last checked Fri 6 Nov 2026). Times and deals can change, so check with the pub before a big night out.

Run a pub or venue? Send your events to smiffscompass@gmail.com and they’ll go on the map for free, and get a free badge for your website.
