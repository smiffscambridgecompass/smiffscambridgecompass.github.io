Cambridge pub ghosts

A window that must never be shut, a landlady who never left, and an old man who vanished on Silver Street. These are the pub ghost stories that come with a source. They're local legend, so believe as much as you like, then go and have a pint and decide for yourself.

The Anchor

Silver Street, CB3 9EL

In 1954 a Queens' College student followed an old man along the street here, only for the figure to vanish when he was two paces away. He reported it to the Society for Psychical Research, whose president found him sincere.

The full story, and what’s on at The Anchor ›

Sources: Capturing Cambridge

The Eagle

8 Bene't Street, CB2 3QN

Three children are said to have died in a fire here around 300 years ago, and a window above the garden is kept permanently open so their spirits can come and go. Close it, the story goes, and the room feels like it's suffocating. Some also tell of two Second World War airmen haunting the place.

The full story, and what’s on at The Eagle ›

Sources: Wikipedia · Historic England · Greene King: haunted pubs

The Pickerel Inn

30 Magdalene Street, CB3 0AF

The pub is said to be haunted by a former landlady who drowned in the Cam. Staff have reported feeling her presence.

Sources: Capturing Cambridge · Historic England · Varsity

Smiff’s Cambridge Compass is a free map of what’s on in Cambridge pubs, made by a local pub-goer. Listings come from venue websites, CAMRA and event listings, and are checked twice a week (last checked Fri 13 Nov 2026). Times and deals can change, so check with the pub before a big night out.
