Smiff’s Cambridge Compass is a free map of what’s on in Cambridge pubs, made by a local pub-goer. Listings come from venue websites, CAMRA and event listings, and are checked twice a week (last checked Fri 13 Nov 2026). Times and deals can change, so check with the pub before a big night out.
